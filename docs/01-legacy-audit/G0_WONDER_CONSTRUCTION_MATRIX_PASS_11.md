## Confirmed cross-city and build-time rules

- [National wonders](https://civ-wiki.vimeworld.com/wiki/Национальные_чудеса) **do not consume normal world wonder slot**; active buffs require relevant buildings to remain in **all cities except captured cities**. Nation choice affects special buildings and bonuses but is a **separate** system.
- [Construction guide](https://civ-wiki.vimeworld.com/wiki/Постройка_зданий) describes global world-wonder uniqueness, announcement of construction/progress, conflict if another civilization finishes first, partial refund (50%) and architecture experience, and destruction during war. Need server-authoritative transactional race resolution on completion.
- Individual wonder pages often state **maximum two ordinary wonders per town**, while the page for **Мировой маяк** in another version states **three**. This is a **version conflict** (`WONDER-CONFLICT-003`), not a number to silently select.
- [World Bank](https://civ-wiki.vimeworld.com/wiki/Всемирный_банк): five upgrade levels 200–400%; bank level 10 host + stock exchange coverage; its victory-relevant level 5 is **not the same as merely building the base wonder**.
- [Space Shuttle](https://civ-wiki.vimeworld.com/wiki/Космический_Шаттл): needs Factory; physical crafting and mission chain; **25,000 coins/day upkeep** is specified. Unique-server limit in card; use distinct ownership/uniqueness policy from ordinary wonders.
- [Burj Al Arab](https://civ-wiki.vimeworld.com/wiki/Бурдж_Аль_Араб): hotel in every city required before construction; downstream culture victory requires native-city cumulative culture.
- [Chichen Itza](https://civ-wiki.vimeworld.com/wiki/Чичен-Ица): `all T3 technologies` as a set requirement (source article). This cannot be expanded into final concrete node IDs until the base tree tier labels are confirmed.
- [Statue of Zeus](https://civ-wiki.vimeworld.com/wiki/Статуя_Зевса): tower-like autonomous combat effect and other war effects; wonder may be an active defense, not only scalar modifier. Keep combat component separate from wonder passive buff.
- [Tree of Life](https://civ-wiki.vimeworld.com/wiki/Древо_Жизни): increases crop growth by +50%, town culture +20%, city improvement capacity +100%, reduces civ upgrade costs 20%; has exclusive trade good. Production modifiers must have explicit source.
- [Parthenon](https://civ-wiki.vimeworld.com/wiki/Парфенон) and [Eiffel Tower](https://civ-wiki.vimeworld.com/wiki/Эйфелева_башня) show different culture/combat impacts; do not assign same generic “wonder culture boost” category.
- [V0.9 patch notes](https://civ-wiki.vimeworld.com/wiki/CivCraft_V0.9) have different Parthenon price and changed Council requirements; **chronological balance data** must be retained and the exact Reborn default selected later.
- Some nation-wide *infrastructure coverage* prerequisites are checked at construction, some bonuses require **continued** coverage; document both as separate conditions instead of one permanent unlock.
- The wiki index of world wonders has exactly 22 names; one technology image row has label spelling variants, not evidence of a different wonder count.
