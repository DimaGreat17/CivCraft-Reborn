## Explicit issues and rules

1. `s_trommel` vs `Дробилка`, `s_store` vs `Торговый центр`: possible naming/behavioral drift, **do not mark as confirmed same building**.
2. `ti_trade_outpost` may relate to later `Плантация`, `Рудник` and aquatic trade-site variants; one-to-many mapping requires rules on ownership, biome and site type.
3. Old `ti_road` and `s_stable` have no identical name on later wiki navigation; this is no evidence of removal.
4. Later military ships, new buildings, national structures and wonders must not be merged into old ordinary structures by similar translation alone.
5. All current classifications are **auditor mappings**, not approved Reborn feature decisions. `KEEP/ADAPT/EXCLUDE` awaits GDD and explicit user approval.
6. Exact old Java source mapping still incomplete for `s_shipyard`, `ti_pasture`, `ti_road` and `ti_wall`.
7. Full later-era technology requirements must not be inferred from era heading. Each unlock needs a card or verified graph edge.
8. Later wiki index was revised after 2022 in some pages; capture article-specific revision rather than relying on one front-page date.
